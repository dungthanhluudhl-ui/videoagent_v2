# Compact material examples

These are reasoning examples, not templates.

## Narrative location becomes confinement

The official record remains factual authority, but the beat asks the viewer to
understand place and physical consequence. Choose a truthful contextual plate or a
clearly labelled photographic reconstruction, then make arrival become confinement.
Do not show the PDF by default and do not substitute icons on a blank board.

## Phone call becomes family pressure

Use truthful contextual/reconstruction material that can show the transfer of pressure
from caller to family without pretending it is authentic case imagery. If no depiction
can avoid fabrication, a document-only treatment may be approved only with that
specific reason recorded in `documentOnlyJustification`.

## Official record proves a claim

Choose a document treatment, lock the authentic page, identify the evidence region and anchor
phrase, and compose the actual raster in PREVIS. PROMOTE may travel focus to that
region. Never retype the sentence into a fake document.

## Route or jurisdiction matters

Choose `map`, bind real map/geographic identity, and annotate only the relevant route
or boundary. A pin on blank paper is not a map.

## Quantitative comparison matters

Choose `chart`, store the exact numeric values and source identity, then use the
semantically suitable bar/line/pie form. Do not chart a lone number merely for variety.

## Abstract legal relation

If actual media, documents, geography, data and a labelled reconstruction cannot
show the relationship, choose `diagram-exception` and state why. Keep it minimal.

## Contextual plate across several beats

One strong real or labelled reconstruction plate can support related narration via
crop, reframe, push, pan, zoom, scrim and document layering. Do not source one filler
image per scene.
